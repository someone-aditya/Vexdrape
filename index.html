01 / The beginning

Wear your perspective.

VEXDRAPE began with a simple idea: streetwear should be more than a trend. It should be a way to express who you are.

Our direction is minimal, considered and expressive. We focus on strong visual identity, everyday pieces and designs that give people room to make their own statement.

We are building this one drop at a time. This is the first perspective.
